The archive is the backfill of all the stories in all of your feeds. If you were to click on All Site Stories, you could scroll, and when you’re done scrolling, that would be every story from every feed ever published. Premium archive accounts also get the luxury of having NewsBlur proactively backfill all the content if possible.
