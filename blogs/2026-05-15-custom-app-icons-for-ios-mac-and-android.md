Someone on the forum posted a long-ish list of ideas , and one of them was small enough that I built it the same week: let people change the NewsBlur app icon. Your home screen is yours, and an app you open dozens of times a day should look the way you want it to. So premium subscribers can now choose a custom app icon on iOS, Mac, and Android.
